# English-language localization and historical provenance

The repository's public-facing model descriptions, charts, executable reports and diagrams are maintained in English.

Research records from October 9, 2026 were initially authored in Portuguese. Translations preserve the scientific claims, numeric results, qualifications and dates **as of the original record**. When these historical notes say a repository, MIT license, CI run or DOI was unavailable, do not treat that wording as an assertion about current availability.

The original Portuguese versions remain in earlier immutable Git commits (for example, `b0716d751b46c82b6029b03a16edcd4962c52c5e`), together with their source manifests. The newer English version is an explanatory translation, not evidence of a new independent review, new forecast freeze or ex-ante publication.

No files in `forecast/` are rewritten during localization. The historical `SHA256SUMS` is an original-version artifact, not a manifest of every later translation or regenerated figure. The current figure hashes are in `results/FIGURE_SHA256SUMS`.

Third-party external documents, historical patches and verbatim source captures should remain attributed to their authors. Translating them into native English would create a derivative; keep the original available through Git history and explicitly label the derivative.
