- **Third-party Python dependencies:** NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and pgmpy each have their own licenses. See their original distribution metadata. The project's MIT license does not override them.

- **Historical contributed artifacts:** material in `evidence/` may have different authorship or licensing provenance. Do not treat the presence of a file as permission to relicense its upstream source.

If a copied image, dataset or source extract lacks documented redistribution rights, open an issue and exclude it from derivative distributions until provenance is clarified.
